The tech behind it

Piezoelectric effect

Squeeze certain ceramics and they release electric charge. Each step bends the layer a few millimetres, enough for a small, usable pulse.

Store and smooth

Each pulse is tiny and spiky. A rectifier and supercapacitor turn it into steady power for LEDs and sensors.

Data on every step

The same signal tells us when someone walked by. That becomes a live footfall dashboard for owners, schools and sponsors.

How a footstep becomes power.

A piezoelectric layer under the tile makes a small voltage when it is squeezed. Stridify stores it and sends it to nearby lights and sensors.
